research

ml systems research

in progress

researcher · mixture-of-experts inference

Conducting collaborative research on MoE inference optimization, with a focus on GPU memory management and decode latency.

I’m developing a vLLM-based implementation of VAMP’s dynamic memory repartitioning framework, exploring how memory allocation can adapt across the different stages of inference.

The work combines predictive expert prefetching and compressed weight transfer to recover degraded decoding performance while preserving improvements in time to first token.
